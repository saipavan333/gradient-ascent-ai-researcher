Your first 20 minutes

A step-by-step script for your very first session. No preparation, no maths, nothing to install. Just follow it in order and stop when the twenty minutes are up.

Day 1 20 minutes Zero background needed
Read this first

You are not going to understand everything today — and that is the plan, not a problem. Today has exactly one job: to show you that this stuff is touchable. You'll play with two things that actually learn in front of you, read a few paragraphs, and stop. That's a complete first day.

0–1 min

Bookmark this site

Save this page. Decide right now when you'll do this each day — the time matters more than the length. After breakfast, after dinner, pick one.

Why: the people who finish aren't the smartest ones, they're the ones with a slot in the day.

1–6 min

Watch a neural network actually learn

Open the Neural Network Playground. Press Train and just watch. Then switch the dataset to spirals and train again. Add a few neurons and try once more.

Notice: nobody ever told it the rule. It found the boundary by nudging numbers up and down, thousands of times. That is the entire trick of machine learning, and you just watched it happen.

6–11 min

Find out how it learns

Open the Gradient Descent lab. Press play and watch the ball roll downhill. Now drag the learning rate slider up, higher and higher, until the whole thing explodes.

Notice: learning is just walking downhill on a landscape, one step at a time. Take steps that are too big and you fly off the mountain. You now understand the single most common reason training fails.

11–17 min

Read the beginning of Lesson 1

Open Track 1, Lesson 1 — Vectors. Read only until you hit the first interactive box, then drag the arrows around for a minute. Then stop, even if you want to keep going.

Why stop: finishing hungry is what makes you come back tomorrow. Bingeing today is what makes you quit on Thursday.

17–19 min

Mark it complete

Hit the Mark complete button at the bottom of the lesson, then open your Progress dashboard.

Your streak now reads 1. That number is going to do more work than motivation ever will.

19–20 min

Close the laptop

You're done. Same time tomorrow, and give it 45 minutes instead of 20.

You've seen a network learn, seen why it sometimes fails, and read your first lesson. That's a genuine day one.

Five rules that matter more than the material

  • Play first, read second. Nearly every lesson has a lab linked at the top. Open it before you read. Intuition first, words second — that order sticks, and the reverse mostly doesn't.
  • Predict before you touch the slider. Say out loud what you think will happen, then do it. Being wrong and noticing is where the actual learning lives — much more than in reading another paragraph.
  • Small and daily beats a heroic weekend. One lesson and one lab a day finishes this course inside a year. Four hours one Sunday and nothing after finishes nothing.
  • Confusion is the feeling of learning. It's a sign you're at the edge of what you know, not a verdict on whether you're clever enough. Sit in it for a few minutes before you reach for help.
  • Stopping partway is still a win. Someone who truly understands the first six tracks is ahead of most people who say they "know AI". This is a gift, not a test you can fail.
