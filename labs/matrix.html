Matrix Transformer

A matrix is a machine that moves space. Steer its four numbers and watch the whole grid — and a little house — stretch, rotate, shear, or flip. The glowing arrows are the eigenvectors: the rare directions the matrix only stretches, never turns.

▸ Do this
Drag the four matrix sliders, or hit a preset (rotate, shear, flip…). Watch the determinant.
◈ You'll see
Space warps with the grid; the house transforms; the gold arrows (eigenvectors) only grow or shrink. When the determinant hits 0, space collapses to a line.

What's happening: every point (x,y) becomes (a·x+b·y, c·x+d·y). Straight lines stay straight and the origin stays put — that's what makes it linear. The determinant is how much area is scaled (negative = space flipped inside-out). The eigenvectors are the axes the transformation acts along. This is Matrices as linear maps and Eigenvectors, live.
