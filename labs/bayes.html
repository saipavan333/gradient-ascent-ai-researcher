Bayes Explorer

Is this coin fair? Start with a belief, flip the coin, and watch your belief update. This is Bayes' rule in one picture: posterior ∝ prior × likelihood — where you end up is a tug-of-war between what you assumed and what you saw.

▸ Do this
Set your prior (how biased you think the coin is, and how sure). Then add heads and tails you've observed.
◈ You'll see
The bright posterior curve slides from your prior toward the data — and gets sharper (more certain) the more flips you add.
belief about the coin's bias p
—
What's happening: each curve is a Beta distribution over the coin's true heads-probability p. The prior is your starting belief; the likelihood is what the flips alone say; the posterior multiplies them and re-normalises. With few flips the prior dominates; pile on evidence and the data wins and the belief narrows to a spike. This is Bayes' rule, live.
