K-Means Clustering

Unsupervised learning you can watch happen. There are no labels — just a cloud of points. K-means finds the groups by itself, two moves at a time: colour each point by its nearest centre, then slide every centre to the middle of its colour.

▸ Do this
Set k (how many clusters), then press Run — or Step to go one move at a time. Click the canvas to add your own points.
◈ You'll see
Points snap to their nearest glowing centre; the centres drift into the heart of each group and lock in place once nothing changes.

Press ▶ Run and watch the centres find the clusters. Try k=3 on a 4-blob cloud — what goes wrong?
What's happening: k-means minimises the total distance from points to their centre by alternating two guaranteed-downhill steps — assign (nearest centre) and update (mean of the group). It always converges, but where it lands depends on the starting centres — press Re-seed a few times and watch it settle differently. This is Classical ML in motion.
