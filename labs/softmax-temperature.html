Softmax Temperature

When a language model picks the next word, one dial controls its whole personality: temperature. Turn it down and the model plays it safe and repetitive; turn it up and it gets wild and creative. Same scores, completely different behaviour.

▸ Do this
Drag temperature and watch the word probabilities. Hit Sample ×20 to actually draw words at that setting.
◈ You'll see
Low T → almost all the probability piles onto the top word (safe, deterministic). High T → the bars flatten toward equal (surprising, risky). The scores never changed — only the temperature.

Slide the temperature and watch the distribution breathe.
What's happening: the model outputs a raw score (logit) per word. Softmax turns scores into probabilities as pi = escorei/T ⁄ Σ escore/T. Dividing by temperature T before exponentiating rescales the gaps: small T magnifies differences (→ near one-hot, greedy), large T shrinks them (→ uniform, random). At T→0 you get argmax; at T→∞ you get a coin flip. This is the sampling knob behind every LLM decoder. Scores here are fixed so you can isolate T's effect.
