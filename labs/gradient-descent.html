Gradient Descent Lab

Three optimizers — plain gradient descent, momentum, and Adam — race downhill across the same loss landscape. Watch who reaches the valley first, who zig-zags, and who overshoots.

▸ Do this
Pick a landscape, press Run, and drag the learning rate higher and higher.
◈ You'll see
Plain descent zig-zags down narrow valleys; momentum and Adam cut straight through. Push the rate too high and they overshoot and explode.
