The Multi-Armed Bandit

Five slot machines, each with a hidden payout rate. Every pull is a choice: cash in on the best one you've found, or gamble on a machine you haven't tried enough. This explore-vs-exploit dilemma is the seed of all reinforcement learning.

▸ Do this
Pick a strategy and press Play to let it pull arms.
◈ You'll see
Pure greedy often locks onto a lucky-looking but sub-optimal arm. ε-greedy and UCB keep sampling and home in on the true best, earning more.

Press Play and watch the strategy figure out which machine pays best.
What's happening: each arm keeps a running estimate of its payout. Greedy trusts those estimates completely and can get stuck; ε-greedy tries a random arm now and then; UCB adds a bonus to arms it's unsure about, so it explores exactly what it hasn't tested enough. Regret — how much reward you lost versus always picking the true best — is the score to minimise. This is Reinforcement Learning's core tension.
