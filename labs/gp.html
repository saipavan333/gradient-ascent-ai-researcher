Gaussian Process: Honest Uncertainty

Most models give you a single guess. A Gaussian process gives you a guess plus how sure it is — confident where it has seen data, and honestly uncertain everywhere else. Click to add points and watch the confidence band breathe.

▸ Do this
Click anywhere to add a data point, and slide the smoothness (length-scale).
◈ You'll see
The shaded band pinches to nothing at every data point and flares wide in the gaps and beyond the edges — the model admitting what it doesn't know.
click to add points · band = ±2σ uncertainty
Click to place a few points and watch the curve thread them with an uncertainty band.
What's happening: a GP treats the unknown function as a distribution over all smooth curves that pass through your points. Its posterior mean is the best single guess; its variance is zero at the data (it must pass through) and grows with distance from it. The length-scale sets how quickly the function is allowed to wiggle. This is Gaussian Processes.
