Attention Visualizer

The idea that made Transformers work: every word can look at every other word and decide who matters. Click a word to see where it pays attention — and switch between "heads" to see that different heads learn completely different jobs.

▸ Do this
Click any word to make it the one doing the looking, and change the head.
◈ You'll see
Glowing arcs reach from your word to the ones it attends to — thicker arc = stronger attention. Each head has its own habit.
click a word · arcs show what it attends to
—
What's happening: attention gives every word a query and every word a key; the match between them (a dot product, softmaxed) becomes the weight of the arc. Stacking many heads lets one look back one word, another find the subject, another track quotes — and the network blends them. This is Self-attention, made visible.
