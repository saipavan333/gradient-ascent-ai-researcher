Neural Network Playground

A neural network built by hand in plain JavaScript — no libraries — learning to tell two classes apart in real time. You choose the shape, the number of neurons, and the learning rate; it does the rest, live.

▸ Do this
Pick a dataset, press Train, then slide the neurons up and down.
◈ You'll see
The glowing boundary bends to wrap the dots. More neurons → curvier fits; too-high a rate → it thrashes.

Press ▶ Train to start. Try the spiral with 16 neurons — it's the hardest to fit.
What's happening: each hidden neuron draws one straight line and bends it with its activation. The network adds those bent lines together into a curved boundary, and backpropagation nudges every weight downhill on the loss until the two colours are cleanly split — the exact maths from Backpropagation, here under your control.
