Neural Network Playground

A neural network — built by hand in plain JavaScript, no libraries — learning to separate two classes in real time. Pick a shape, choose how many hidden neurons it gets, set the learning rate, and press Train. Watch the coloured decision boundary bend itself around the data.

🔬 Interactive Lab · watch a network learn a boundary
Blue vs. orange dots are the two classes. The shaded background is what the network currently believes — where it's unsure, the colour is pale. Training nudges it until the boundary fits.

💡 What you're actually watching

Each hidden neuron draws one straight line and bends it with its activation. With more neurons, the network can combine more bent lines into a curvy boundary. Too few neurons and it can't capture the shape; too high a learning rate and it thrashes. This is the same maths from Deep Learning from Scratch — here you get to drive it.
