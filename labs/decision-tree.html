Decision Tree: Carving the Plane

A decision tree classifies by asking a cascade of yes/no questions, each one a straight cut. Grow it deeper and watch it slice the space into ever-finer boxes — until it's memorised every last point.

▸ Do this
Slide the depth from 1 to 8. Generate new data to see it adapt.
◈ You'll see
Each level adds axis-aligned cuts, carving rectangles coloured by their majority class. Shallow = simple; deep = jagged boxes hugging every point (overfitting).
—leaf boxes

What's happening: at each node the tree picks the single vertical or horizontal cut that best separates the two classes (lowest Gini impurity), then repeats inside each half. Because every cut is axis-aligned, the regions are always rectangles. A deep tree reaches 100% training accuracy by boxing in every point — which is exactly why a single tree overfits, and why random forests average many. This is Decision Trees.
