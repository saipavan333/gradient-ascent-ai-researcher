GAN: The Forger's Game

Two networks locked in a duel. A generator tries to forge samples that look real; a discriminator tries to spot the fakes. As they fight, the forgeries get better and better — until they're indistinguishable from the real thing.

▸ Do this
Press Train. Drag the real data somewhere new and watch the generator chase it.
◈ You'll see
The orange fake distribution slides and reshapes until it sits right on top of the blue real one, and the discriminator's confidence flattens to a coin-flip.

blue = real · orange = generated · violet = discriminator
Press ▶ Train — the generator starts clueless and learns to mimic the real distribution.
What's happening: the generator maps noise to samples with a mean and spread it can tune; the discriminator is a classifier trying to separate real from fake. Each step, the discriminator sharpens and the generator moves to fool it — a min-max game whose equilibrium is fake = real, where the discriminator can only guess (50%). This is GANs, in one dimension.
