Word Embeddings & Analogies

Words become points in space, arranged so that directions carry meaning. The step from "man" to "woman" is the same arrow as "king" to "queen" — which means you can do analogies with plain arithmetic on vectors.

▸ Do this
Build an analogy: pick A − B + C (e.g. king − man + woman).
◈ You'll see
The arrows are parallel, and the result lands on the answer (queen). The same direction encodes "gender", "royalty", or "plural".
—A − B + C ≈
directions = meaning · gold = the analogy result
—
What's happening: a model like word2vec learns a vector for every word by predicting its neighbours in text. Words used in similar contexts end up close together, and consistent relationships (gender, tense, capital-of) become consistent directions. So "king − man + woman" literally travels the gender arrow from king and lands near queen. This is the intuition behind token embeddings.
