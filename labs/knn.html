k-Nearest Neighbours

The lazy classifier: it learns nothing in advance. To label a new point, it simply asks the k closest examples to vote. Drag the query around and watch its verdict flip as it crosses between neighbourhoods.

▸ Do this
Drag the white query dot across the plane and change k.
◈ You'll see
Lines shoot to the k nearest points that vote on the class. Small k → a jagged, noise-sensitive boundary; large k → a smooth, blurry one.
drag the white dot · lines = the k voters
—
What's happening: the shaded background shows how every point in the plane would be classified — the k-NN decision boundary. It has no equation; it falls straight out of where the training points sit. Tiny k hugs every point (low bias, high variance); big k averages over a wide neighbourhood (smoother, but it can wash out real structure). This is k-Nearest Neighbours.
