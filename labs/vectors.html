Vectors & the Dot Product

Almost everything in machine learning is a vector, and almost every question about two vectors — how aligned are they? how much does one point along the other? — is answered by one operation: the dot product. Drag the arrows and watch it come alive.

▸ Do this
Drag the tip of either arrow. Try making them point the same way, opposite ways, and at a right angle.
◈ You'll see
The dot product is positive when they agree, negative when they oppose, and exactly zero when they're perpendicular. The gold segment is a's shadow on b — the projection.
—a · b (dot product)
—angle between them
blue = a · teal = b · gold = projection of a onto b · drag the tips
Drag an arrow tip to begin.
What's happening: the dot product is a · b = axbx + ayby, and it also equals |a| |b| cos θ where θ is the angle between the arrows and |v| is a vector's length. So the sign of the dot product is the sign of cos θ: same direction → positive, opposite → negative, perpendicular → zero. Dividing out the lengths gives cosine similarity — the workhorse behind matching embeddings, attention scores, and similarity search. The projection of a onto b has length (a·b)/|b| and shows "how much of a points along b." This is Vectors.
